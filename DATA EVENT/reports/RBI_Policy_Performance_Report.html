1. Core Algorithmic Logic — Buy-Before / Sell-After MPC Model

The system is non-predictive positioning around a scheduled regulatory event. Rather than forecasting the rate action, it anchors to the RBI MPC decision day T and uses each stock's multi-cycle base rate to enter ahead of the announcement and exit after the post-decision drift completes.

The Two-Leg Trade Structure

  • Leg 1 — Buy Before: enter N trading days before decision day T at 09:20 AM, as institutions position into the policy.
  • Leg 2 — Hold Through & Sell After: hold across the decision and exit M trading days after T at 03:15 PM, capturing the full post-policy drift.

Trade Return: Returni = ((Pexit, T+M − Pentry, T−N) / Pentry, T−N) × Directioni, where Direction = +1 (LONG) or −1 (SHORT), and T is the NSE trading day of the RBI MPC decision.

6. Capital Deployment, Margin Rules & Execution Playbook

1. Anchor & Non-Predictive Positioning

T is the RBI MPC decision/announcement day (09-Oct-2026 (Friday 10:00 AM)). The model does not forecast the rate action — it positions around the scheduled decision using each stock's empirical pre/post-decision drift, and tilts the window by scenario (cut / hike / status-quo) where history warrants.

2. Execution Timings

  • Entry: 09:20 AM on trading day T−N (limit / market-at-open).
  • Exit: 03:10–03:25 PM on trading day T+M, marking the full post-decision drift.

3. Position Sizing & Margin

Real NSE futures lot sizes, one lot per stock; 20% SPAN + Exposure initial margin per position.

4. Options Alternative & Stop-Loss

For capital-efficient deployment, buy near-month ATM/ITM Calls (LONG bias) or Puts (SHORT bias), capping risk to premium paid. Hard stop-loss of −3.5% on spot guards against outlier policy-shock reversals.
