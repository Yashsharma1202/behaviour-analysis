1. Core Algorithmic Logic — Buy-Before / Sell-After Holiday Model

The system is non-predictive positioning around a scheduled exchange holiday. Rather than forecasting news, it anchors to the fixed NSE Dussehra / Vijayadashami holiday date and uses each stock's own multi-year base rate to enter ahead of the holiday and exit after the post-holiday drift completes.

The Two-Leg Trade Structure

  • Leg 1 — Buy Before (Anticipation Run-Up): enter N trading days before the holiday date T at 09:20 AM, as participants position into the festive cycle.
  • Leg 2 — Hold Through & Sell After (Digest): hold across the closed session and exit M trading days after T at 03:15 PM, capturing the full post-holiday drift.

Trade Return: Returni = ((Pexit, T+M − Pentry, T−N) / Pentry, T−N) × Directioni, where Direction = +1 (LONG) or −1 (SHORT), and T is the NSE trading day of the Dussehra holiday.

5. Capital Deployment, Margin Rules & Execution Playbook

1. Position Sizing & Margin

Sizing adheres to real NSE futures lot sizes, one lot per stock. Margin is the exchange 20% initial requirement (SPAN + Exposure), aggregating to ~₹72.2 Lakhs of deployed capital for the full 50-stock cycle.

2. Execution Timings

  • Entry: 09:20 AM on trading day T−N (limit / market-at-open).
  • Exit: 03:10–03:25 PM on trading day T+M. The position is held straight through the exchange holiday.

3. Non-Predictive Anchor

T is the NSE trading day of the Dussehra / Vijayadashami holiday (the exchange is closed that day). The model positions around the holiday liquidity / festive-cycle drift rather than forecasting news.

4. Options Alternative & Stop-Loss

For capital-efficient deployment, buy near-month ATM/ITM Calls (LONG bias) or Puts (SHORT bias), capping risk to premium paid. A hard stop-loss of −3.5% on spot guards against outlier holiday-window shocks.
