Methodology — How Each Measure Is Calculated

MeasureLogicFormula
Window & eventEach stock has its own T-n/T+m trading-day window around the 20-Oct-2026 Dussehra anchor, on the NSE-2026 calendar.Entry = anchor − n trading days; Exit = anchor + m trading days.
Split-robust returnDaily %% changes inside the window are compounded; any single-day move >20%% (stock split/bonus artifact) is skipped.year_return = (Π(1+daily_%%), skip |daily_%%|>20%%) − 1, ×100.
Direction (LONG/SHORT)Both directions backtested on the same yearly moves; the one with the higher win rate across all years is chosen per stock.Chosen = direction with max(LONG win%%, SHORT win%%).
Win Rate %%Share of years the chosen direction was profitable.winning years ÷ total years with data × 100.
Avg Return %%Simple average of the signed yearly returns.mean(signed yearly returns).
CAGR %%Compounded annual growth if only this one trade/year were reinvested year over year — rewards consistency over lucky one-offs.(Π(1+r/100))^(1/n) − 1, ×100.
Max Drawdown %%Peak-to-trough decline on the cumulative equity curve of the yearly trades (trade-level, not intraday).min((equity_t − running_peak) ÷ running_peak) ×100.
Sharpe-likeRisk-adjusted return vs. year-to-year variability; NOT annualized — only compare within this report.mean(yearly returns) ÷ population_stdev(yearly returns).
Confidence & this report’s filterOK = ≥3 years of data (LOW excluded entirely here). This report keeps only Confidence=OK AND Win Rate≥70%%, ranks by avg return, shows the top 50.59 stocks pass the filter → top 50 by avg_return shown.
