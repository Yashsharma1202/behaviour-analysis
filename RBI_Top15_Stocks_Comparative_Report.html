3. How to read this & caveats

Selection: these are the most consistent RBI-drift names, but the edge is modest (win rates 56–64%, median +0.5–+2%) and drawdowns are large at the single-name level (‒30% to ‒82%) — individual stocks are far more volatile than the index basket. Best risk-adjusted: the Healthcare names (APOLLOHOSP, DIVISLAB) carry the highest profit factors (~2.0). Scenario matters: as with the index, the edge concentrates on a rate pause; going long into a rate cut is weak. Trade these as a diversified basket, size for the drawdowns, and prefer the pause meetings.
