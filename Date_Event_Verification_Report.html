Three mistakes I made in this audit, each caught only by independently re-verifying my own work instead of trusting my first pass:
  1. I initially reported "all 19 fixed," but only 11 had actually been written back (the other 8 — KOTAKBANK, INDUSINDBK ×2, HEROMOTOCO ×2, SUNPHARMA, ULTRACEMCO, TATASTEEL — were found but never saved). Caught by independently re-reading every record and comparing its stored price against the real price on its stored date — found 8 that didn't match, then fixed them for real.
  2. The 211-stock Diwali-2025 price recomputation used a holiday calendar that only covers 2026, so it didn't know 22-Oct-2025 (Balipratipada) was a market holiday — 33 of 211 exit dates landed on a day with no real trading. Added the correct 2025 holiday dates and recomputed all 211 again; now 421/422 checks pass (the 1 exception is the already-documented TATAMOTORS delisting gap).
  3. ULTRACEMCO's "fix" was itself wrong. NSE's own feed has two broadcast entries for its Jan-Mar 2024 quarter — 29-Apr-2024 and 03-May-2024, both marked Audited (almost certainly the original results announcement followed days later by a formal re-filing of the same already-approved numbers). My matching code took whichever row appeared first in the file without checking for a second match, and "corrected" the already-correct 29-Apr-2024 to 03-May-2024 — a real NSE date, just the wrong one, since the market reacts to the first disclosure, not a later re-filing of the same numbers. Found by printing the raw NSE source rows for every one of the 19 fixes and reading them myself rather than trusting the script's single-row match; reverted to 29-Apr-2024 with prices recomputed accordingly. Then scanned the entire dataset for this same duplicate-filing pattern — confirmed ULTRACEMCO was the only instance.
136 records remain honestly flagged synthetic_placeholder_unverified in the data itself (that flag already existed before I started — the project's own pipeline knew these weren't verified) — NSE's live feed only keeps roughly the last 4-5 quarters per stock, so these have aged out of what I can check this way. Not guessed, not left silently wrong — left exactly as self-flagged. A further 596 stock-quarter combinations (back to mid-2022) have no live source to check against at all.
