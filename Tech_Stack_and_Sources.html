01Methods — the techniques we used

The system is built on plain statistics and exhaustive search — nothing learned or fitted.

MethodWhat it doesUsed in
Event-study windowMeasures price change in a window around each corporate event.event_behaviour.py
Exhaustive grid searchTests all 64 buy-before / sell-after timing rules (N,M = 1…8) — no optimiser, just try them all.event_behaviour.py
Descriptive statisticsWin rate (share of events that finished positive), average, median, best/worst case.event_behaviour.py · stock_server.py
Trading-day indexingCounts in market days, skipping weekends & holidays.event_behaviour.py
Market adjustmentSubtracts the NIFTY index move to isolate stock-specific reaction.nifty_backtest.py · event_study.py
Train / test + placeboSplits history and tests random dates to check an edge isn't just curve-fitting.nifty_backtest.py
Fingerprinted disk cachePersists rankings/screener keyed by a data fingerprint → instant loads, auto-refresh on change.stock_server.py
